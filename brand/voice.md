# Orion — Name, Voice & Copy

---

## 1. The name

**Orion.** Named for the mythic hunter and the constellation that bears his name — a
navigational anchor people have used to orient themselves in the dark for millennia.
The hunter never stops seeking; neither does the product.

- Written `Orion`. Never `ORION`, never `orion` in prose (lowercase is fine in code, domains, package names).
- The full entity is **Orion Protocol**. `Est. 2026`.

## 3. Boilerplate

> Navigate the global scientific corpus as a spatial manifold. Orion synthesizes
> distinct research paradigms to identify sparse regions and compute undiscovered
> hypotheses in real time.

## 4. Manifesto

> Named for a hunter who never stops **seeking**.
> We built an engine for **endless discovery**.

## 5. The three pillars

**01 · Limitless Scale** — Like the cosmos, knowledge expands continuously. The manifold
architecture scales infinitely, mapping the entire scientific corpus without loss of
topological precision.

**02 · Infinite Density** — Where ideas converge, a singularity of understanding forms.
We compress the collective intelligence of humanity into dense vectorial manifolds that
surface hidden adjacencies.

**03 · Mysterious Depths** — True discovery lies in the unknown. We chart the dark matter
of science — the missing hypotheses and unasked questions — and render them navigable
for the first time.

## 6. The thesis quote

> Space has no limits. As *profoundly mysterious* and dense as a black hole, it is within
> these boundless regions that true breakthroughs are forged.
> — **The Orion Thesis**

## 7. Tone

**Sound like:** an observatory's research notes. Precise, unhurried, physical. Technical
vocabulary used correctly — *manifold, topology, corpus, vector, sparse region,
adjacency, node, edge*. Short declaratives. Cosmic metaphors that are earned, not
decorative.

**Don't sound like:** a SaaS launch. No "revolutionary", "game-changing", "unlock",
"supercharge", "10x", "AI-powered". No exclamation marks. No emoji in product copy. No
second-person hype ("You'll love…").

**Sentence rhythm:** alternate a long compound sentence with a very short one. The short
one carries the claim.

## 8. Copy patterns

- **Section labels** are mono comments: `// The Mark`, `// Manifesto`, `// Core Pillars`. The `//` is always there.
- **Eyebrows** are uppercase, tracked, with a middot separator: `EST. 2026 · BRAND IDENTITY`.
- **Enumeration** is zero-padded: `01`, `02`, `03`.
- **Metrics** pair a Playfair figure with a mono uppercase caption: `1.4B` / `KNOWLEDGE NODES INDEXED`; `1024` / `DIMENSIONAL MANIFOLD SPACE`; `∞` / `SCALE OF HUMAN DISCOVERY`.
- **Separator glyph** in marquees and lockups: `✦`.
- **Emphasis** is a single italic word inside an otherwise roman headline — see [typography.md](typography.md#2-the-italic-rule).
- **Footers and end cards** close with `Orion Protocol · Est. 2026`.
